An Occasionally Illustrated Read-Along Podcast for Capital Volume 1, run by [Hagen Blix](https://hagenblix.github.io/). The podcast got started in August 2026, and trails an in-person reading group I'm facilitating.

The modest goal of this podcast is to help you, the listeners, to get an intellectual skill set out of engaging with this book, one that will help you develop a deeper understanding of the political situations you find yourself in - from the global context down to concrete organizing situations. This is a book one can *think with*, and that's what I want to get across.

I will try my best to make this podcast one that you could listen to without reading, but fundamentally it is a read-along podcast. Every episode will cover a certain page range (indicated in the episode title), and you'll get the most ouf of it, if you follow along! The book is following the new 2024 [Paul Reitter translation](https://bookshop.org/a/114797/9780691190075).

The podcast episodes will come in two flavors -- A-Side and B-Side.

On the A-Side episodes, I'll walk you through the week's reading, aiming to make it both accessible and fruitful. On the B-Side episodes I invite friends, comrades, fellow organizers, and scholars to chat about the reading with me. Sometimes these will be expansive, taking a particular topic to novel realms relevant to life and politics today, and sometimes they'll dive deep into particular aspects of Marx's analysis. Either way, I hope they will all be fun!
